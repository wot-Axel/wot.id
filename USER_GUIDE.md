# WOT.ID User Guide

## Data Storage Update: Migration to Local Storage

### What Has Changed

WOT.ID has recently undergone a significant upgrade to its data storage system. We've implemented encrypted local storage to provide you with a more reliable, faster, and more secure experience.

### What This Means For You

**Better Performance**: Local storage offers improved data access speeds, making the application more responsive.

**Improved Reliability**: The new storage system is more stable and less prone to connection issues.

**Enhanced Security**: Your data remains private and secure, with the same encryption standards as before.

**No Data Loss**: All your existing data has been carefully migrated to the new system without any loss of information.

### Using the Application

You can continue to use WOT.ID exactly as before. All your data - including profiles, digital assets, real-world assets, human relationships, and organizational affiliations - is accessible through the same interface.

### Sections Overview

#### Profile Information
Store and manage your personal identity information securely.

#### Digital Assets
Track your digital possessions, including cryptocurrencies, NFTs, and online accounts.

#### Real-World Assets
Maintain records of physical assets such as property, vehicles, and valuables.

#### Human Relationships
Keep track of your personal and professional connections.

#### Organizational Affiliations
Manage your memberships and associations with various organizations.

#### Trust Balance
View and manage trust attestations through the Ethereum Attestation Service.

### Data Privacy

Your data privacy remains our top priority:

1. **Local Storage**: Your data is primarily stored in your browser's local storage.
2. **Blockchain Integration**: When you choose to connect your wallet, data can be stored on the blockchain.
3. **End-to-End Encryption**: All sensitive data is encrypted before storage.
4. **User Control**: You maintain full control over your data at all times.

### Feedback

If you encounter any issues or have suggestions for improvements, please contact our support team. We're committed to providing you with the best possible experience.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will I notice any differences in how the application works?

Most users will only notice improved performance and reliability. The user interface and functionality remain the same.

### Do I need to take any action regarding my data?

No, all your data has been automatically migrated to the new system. You can continue using the application as usual.

### Is my data still private and secure?

Yes, we maintain the same high standards for data privacy and security. Your data remains encrypted and under your control.

### Can I export my data?

Yes, the data export functionality continues to work as before, allowing you to download your information in a portable format.

### What if I encounter issues after the migration?

If you experience any problems, please contact our support team. We're here to help ensure a smooth transition.
